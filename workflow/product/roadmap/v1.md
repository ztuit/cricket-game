# Product Roadmap — Cricket Game v1
**Status:** draft
**Date:** 2026-08-20

---

## MVP 1 — "The First Ball"
**Goal:** Prove the core loop works — a cricket fan can face a delivery, make a meaningful shot selection, and get a satisfying outcome.

**Stakeholder benefit:** The cricket fan gets to test their knowledge in a real match scenario. The creator validates that the core mechanic is fun before investing in the full game.

**What is included:**
- Single T20 match (20 overs, 10 wickets)
- Toss mechanic (set target or chase)
- Delivery-by-delivery batting: field display, ball information (line, length, pace, limited spin), shot selection, wrist angle dial
- Outcome probability model (shot suitability, surface conditions, bowler characteristics)
- Surface physics: pitch variation by location, basic weather effect, ball age effect
- At least 2 bowler types (e.g., fast and spin) with personality and character cards
- Real ground with weather conditions
- Basic difficulty progression within the match (coefficients shift)
- Runs, wickets, boundaries (4 and 6), wides, no-balls
- Score display and match result (win/loss)
- Minimal team tracking: number of games played, experience accumulated
- Offline, single-player only

**What is not yet included:**
- Career / team progression deeper mechanics (league, campaign, player collection — MVP 2/3)
- Freemium lock or payment integration
- Multiple grounds or team selection
- Player cards for batsmen (bowler cards only in MVP 1)
- Full set of bowler types
- Visual polish / animations beyond functional prototype
- Onboarding or tutorial
- Online features
- Partnership mechanics (YES/NO/WAIT calling, run judging) — future increment
- Fielding side gameplay — future increment

**Validation:** A cricket fan plays one full T20 match and says: "The decisions felt real. I knew why I got out, and I want to try again." The creator can play 5 matches in a row without the loop feeling repetitive.

**Dependencies:** DDD domain model, Senior Engineer technology choice, UX visual style decision.

## MVP 2 — "The Characters"
**Goal:** Make the game characterful — the fictional players with nicknames, quirks, and personality cards are a first-class feature, not an afterthought.

**Stakeholder benefit:** The cricket fan gets entertainment from the players' personalities, not just the cricket mechanics. The casual observer has something to enjoy even without deep cricket knowledge.

**What is included:**
- Full bowler roster: all planned bowler types with distinct characteristics
- Batsman character cards (nickname, quirks, cricket stats)
- Player card UI accessible at any time during play
- At least 3 grounds with distinct weather conditions
- Expanded shot set (curated to ~8–12 shots covering all categories)
- Match-level difficulty tuned and validated
- Visual style committed and implemented (comic book or 2D top-down — decided in MVP 1 prototyping)

**What is not yet included:**
- Career / team progression
- Freemium lock
- Online features

**Validation:** A cricket fan opens a bowler's card, reads their nickname and quirk, and laughs. They then adjust their shot selection because of what they learned about the bowler's type. The character system is influencing gameplay, not just decoration.

**Dependencies:** MVP 1 core loop validated. Visual style decision made (prototyped in MVP 1 or alongside it).

---

## MVP 3 — "The Career"
**Goal:** Give the player a reason to come back — a team to build, a career to progress, and increasingly tough opponents.

**Stakeholder benefit:** The cricket fan has a long-term goal beyond individual matches. The creator has retention mechanics that drive engagement.

**What is included:**
- Team progression system (mechanism TBD — league, campaign, or player collection)
- Career-level difficulty scaling (tougher opponents as team advances)
- Match history / results tracking
- At least 5 grounds
- Freemium lock implemented (free tier: limited matches per session/day; paid tier: unlimited)
- In-app purchase integration (Google Play)

**What is not yet included:**
- Online leaderboards
- Player accounts / cloud saves
- Multiplayer

**Validation:** A cricket fan plays 10+ matches over multiple sessions and feels their team improving. They pay for the full experience because the free tier gave them a taste they want more of. Conversion rate on freemium is measurable.

**Dependencies:** MVP 2 validated. Freemium model decided. Google Play IAP integration.

---

## MVP 4 — "The Arena"
**Goal:** Take the game online — leaderboards, accounts, and competitive play.

**Stakeholder benefit:** The cricket fan can compare their skills against others. The creator has network effects driving retention and acquisition.

**What is included:**
- Player accounts (Google Sign-In or equivalent)
- Cloud saves / cross-device play
- Online leaderboards (by ground, by team, global)
- Match replay sharing

**What is not yet included:**
- Real-time multiplayer
- Community features (forums, chat)

**Validation:** A cricket fan checks the leaderboard after a match and sees where they rank. They share a replay with a friend who downloads the game.

**Dependencies:** MVP 3 validated. Server infrastructure. Account system.

## Roadmap notes

**Visual style prototyping** is deliberately placed as a parallel activity during MVP 1 — not a separate MVP. The core loop can be built with placeholder graphics, and the visual style decision is made based on prototyping real screens with real cricket scenarios. This is a technology decision the UX Expert and Senior Engineer own, with the human making the final call.

**Freemium model** is deferred to MVP 3 because: (a) the core loop must be validated before monetising it, and (b) the free tier design depends on understanding what makes the game compelling enough to convert. The architecture must support IAP from the start, but the lock mechanics are a product decision made after observing real play behaviour.

**Team progression** is the biggest unknown. The mechanism (league, campaign, card collection, or hybrid) will be shaped by the domain model and the human's vision. MVP 3 is the earliest it appears, giving two full MVPs of core gameplay validation first.
