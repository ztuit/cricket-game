##Cricket game

I have an idea for a game that could be played on a phone based on cricket.

#Overview
The idea is to get players of cricket to think about what shot to play based on the field and the line and length of the ball.

The flow would be like this:

Player see how the field it arranged, this might be auto generated, or be driven from rules, sees the stats of the bowler and then elects to be ready to be bowled at.

The display shows the balls like and why it will land also information about it's pace, some spin details but these will be limited to make it harder for the batsman, some coefficient of the surface that changes over time will influence how the ball bounces up.

The player then has a limited time, seconds to choose the type of shot they want to play from a menu. A badly chosen shot for the ball would make the probabilty of hitting the ball lower, the coefficients of the ball and surface will also impact how the ball is hit and where to. The player will also choose the angle of their wrists when they hit the ball.

For example, if the player elects to plat a backfoot shot when the ball full and on stumps would result in a wicket, or a backfoot shot to a spinner with good length might result in being caught in the slips if the ball is spin and the fielders are present there.

Balls will be bowled by batsmen with specific characteristics to keep consistency in overs, and the played at specific grounds with weather conditions to introduce variables.

Balls will be more difficult to play as the game progresses.

#Meta details
This could be played visually, i can also imagine it would work with text, although difficult to describe the field.

I think building for android phones initially would be easier.
